Deposit explanation to know how it works.

The $1,500 deposit acts strictly as a security net for backdated transactions. Sometimes pumps have errors and transactions clear late. This deposit ensures all delayed charges are covered, guaranteeing you never get a surprise bill that ruins your credit score. If you ever cancel, we wait 45 days to refund it simply to ensure all delayed charges have cleared.

So, from the day you decide to request the deposit back, write an email to our accounting department at accounting@fulltank.us and ask to freeze your credit line and request the deposit back. From that day, Full Tank will countdown 45 days and then send you the deposit back.

Flexible Split Payments

We know tying up cash can be tough. We offer flexible installment options. You can pay your deposit in three weekly installments of $500.
